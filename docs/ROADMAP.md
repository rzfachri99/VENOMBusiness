# VENOMBusiness Roadmap

## v0.1 — Foundation ✅
Go API, React/TypeScript UI, SQLite, migrations, security middleware.

## v0.2 — Identity & CRM foundation ✅
Authentication, company setup, RBAC and customer management.

## v0.3 — Commerce Core ✅
Products/services, quotations, invoices and payments.

## v0.4 — Finance & Documents ✅
Business profile, expenses, finance snapshot, quotation/invoice/receipt PDFs and database driver boundary.

## v0.5 — Dual Database & Production Hardening
Full PostgreSQL compatibility, driver-aware SQL/transactions, per-dialect migrations, SQLite/PostgreSQL CI matrix, backup/restore and SQLite → PostgreSQL migration tooling.

## v0.6 — Inventory & CRM Pipeline
Stock movements, warehouses/basic stock, lead/deal pipeline and activities.

## Later
POS, HR, Helpdesk, integrations, plugin/module SDK and optional cloud services.
