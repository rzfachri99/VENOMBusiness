# VENOMBusiness v0.5.1-alpha

This is the first GitHub-focused public alpha preparation release of VENOMBusiness, an open-source, self-hosted Business OS for small businesses and startups.

## What is already available

- Authentication and business onboarding
- Customers
- Products and services
- Quotations
- Invoices and payments
- Expenses and financial summaries
- Business profile
- PDF business documents
- SQLite and PostgreSQL database paths
- Backup/restore and migration utilities

## What this release focuses on

`v0.5.1-alpha` intentionally freezes new business features and focuses on repository quality, public documentation, contribution workflows, security guidance, and release hygiene.

## Important alpha notice

This is pre-1.0 software under active development. Back up your data, test upgrades, and do not rely on VENOMBusiness as the sole record for critical financial information.
