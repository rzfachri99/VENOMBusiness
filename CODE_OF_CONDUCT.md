# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want VENOMBusiness to be a welcoming, professional open-source community. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully regardless of experience level, background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect boundaries, and help keep technical discussions useful to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, personal attacks, or publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to `SECURITY.md`, not discussed publicly before a fix is available.
